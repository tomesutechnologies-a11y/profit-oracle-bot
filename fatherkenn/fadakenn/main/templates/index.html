About Me

Mental Health Advocacy As the convener of Mind and Space

A virtual mental health community, MerciRick provides a safe haven for individuals to share experiences and find solace. Through platforms like WhatsApp and Instagram, the community engages in activities ranging from open discussions on wellness to interactive games designed to alleviate stress. Since its inception in 2021, Mind and Space has spearheaded annual campaigns such as "Help Fight Depression," "30-Day Wellness Challenge," and "Understanding Anxiety." In 2025, the initiative is set to expand into schools, introducing reflection sessions aimed at helping students and educators manage stress while pursuing their passions.

Visual Media Consultancy and Creation

In the realm of visual media, MerciRick combines her passion for photography with entrepreneurial acumen. She offers consultancy services and conducts free periodic classes for young girls and youths, positioning photography as both a stress relief mechanism and a viable career path. Her portfolio spans documentaries, nature photography, and portraiture, capturing the essence of diverse subjects.

Mental Health Advocacy

Creating Spaces for Healing

My journey in mental health advocacy began with my own experiences navigating anxiety and depression. Through creative expression and community building, I've found powerful ways to transform personal struggles into collective healing.

I believe that creativity and mental wellness are deeply interconnected. Art, design, and storytelling can serve as therapeutic tools, helping individuals process emotions and find their voice. My work focuses on creating accessible resources and safe spaces where people can explore their mental health without judgment.
