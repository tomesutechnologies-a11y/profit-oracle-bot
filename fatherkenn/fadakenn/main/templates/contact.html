Frequently Asked Questions

What services do you offer?
I offer a range of creative services including brand identity design, digital experiences, creative direction for campaigns, and mental health advocacy workshops. Each project is tailored to meet your specific needs and goals.
How long does a typical project take?
Project timelines vary depending on scope and complexity. A brand identity project typically takes 4-6 weeks, while digital experiences may take 8-12 weeks. I'll provide a detailed timeline during our initial consultation.
Do you offer mental health workshops?
Yes! I conduct workshops that combine creative expression with mental health awareness. These sessions are designed for both individuals and organizations looking to foster creativity while promoting mental wellness.
What is your pricing structure?
Pricing varies based on project scope and duration. I offer both project-based pricing and retainer options for ongoing creative direction. Contact me for a personalized quote based on your specific needs.
